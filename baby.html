Runs in your browser. Global (Needleman–Wunsch) or local (Smith–Waterman), with a linear gap penalty. The scoring matrix can be downloaded as an Excel file, or you can upload your own Excel (.xlsx) or CSV matrix.

7. Interpreting your results




BLAST hits
  • Low E-value and high bit score
  • Check query coverage, not only % identity
  • Below ~25–30% identity, confirm homology with domains or structure


Alignments
  • Look for conserved blocks and catalytic residues
  • Gap-heavy columns often mean unreliable regions
  • Test matrix and gap sensitivity


Trees
  • Bootstrap ≥ 70–95 suggests solid support
  • Root with an outgroup
  • Distinguish orthologs from paralogs


Structures
  • RMSD is low for similar folds; TM-score > 0.5 suggests the same fold
  • pLDDT > 90 is high confidence; < 50 is likely disordered
  • Check resolution of experimental models


GO / networks
  • Use FDR-corrected enrichment values
  • Use a sensible background gene set
  • Raise the STRING confidence threshold to cut noise
